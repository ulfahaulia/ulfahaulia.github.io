03. Color Palette & HTML Color Codes

Our color palette combines soft pastel shades and natural tones to communicate warmth, elegance, comfort, and a cozy atmosphere. Each color has a specific role in the website's visual design.

04. Design Concept & Credits

The visual identity of Lumière & Lullaby was developed around the concept of softness, warmth, and everyday comfort. The website combines a pastel color palette, rounded elements, and elegant typography to create a cohesive brand experience.

Design Statement

The color palette was selected and arranged as part of the original visual identity of Lumière & Lullaby. Each color was chosen to represent warmth, elegance, comfort, and a soft aesthetic.

The website layout, HTML structure, and CSS styling were developed as part of an Informatics learning project.
