Welcome to Lumière and Lullaby, where tranquility meets gentle elegance. We craft handcrafted scented candles, soothing mists, and self-care essentials to turn your daily routine into a cozy ritual.

Our Profile

Lumière and Lullaby was born out of a passion for peaceful moments amidst a busy world. "Lumière" represents soft, uplifting light, while "Lullaby" signifies the quiet comfort of nighttime relaxation.

Established with love, our atelier focuses on small-batch production to guarantee that every single item carries warm intent, beauty, and premium quality.
