A Lullaby for Your Senses

Founded in 2024, Lumière N Lullaby was born from a simple belief: in an ever-accelerating world, quiet stillness is the ultimate luxury. Our candles are meticulously hand-crafted in small batches to ensure unmatched fragrance throw and burn aesthetic.

Whether you seek peaceful sleep after a long evening, focused clarity during morning meditation, or a romantic ambiance under starry skies, our signature scents are your poetic companion.
